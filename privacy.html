Website privacy.

How we handle inquiries, cookies, and advertising preferences.

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

Project inquiries

The inquiry tool prepares an email draft on your device. Preparing a draft does not submit your message to Prime Reliability Group or a form database. The message is sent when you choose to send it through your email provider.

Use the direct email link if you prefer. Please avoid including sensitive equipment records or confidential documents in an initial inquiry; we can discuss a suitable way to share project information.

Cookies and advertising preferences

We use a first-party cookie to remember your cookie choices. When your browser permits it, this preference cookie is saved after you accept advertising, reject advertising, or save your settings. Rejecting advertising does not prevent this preference cookie from being saved.

After you make a choice, we store one first-party cookie named prg_cookie_preferences for up to 180 days. It records your advertising preference, the preference version and configuration, and its expiry. It contains no visitor ID or contact details and is not used to track you across websites.

Advertising cookies are not currently active

Advertising tracking is currently disabled for every visitor, including visitors who select “Accept advertising.” No advertising or analytics platforms are connected, and this website does not load advertising tags, retargeting pixels, or third-party analytics. Accepting advertising currently saves your preference only.

If we add an advertising provider, we will identify the provider, its purposes, and its cookies here and ask you to make a new choice before enabling its advertising tags.

You can reject advertising or change your preference at any time using Cookie settings. Clearing this site’s cookies also clears your saved choice. If cookies or JavaScript are blocked, optional advertising remains off.

Fonts and visual assets are served with the website. Hosting and email providers may process technical information under their own policies.

Contact

For questions about information you share with Prime Reliability Group, contact Inquiry@primereliabilitygroup.com.
